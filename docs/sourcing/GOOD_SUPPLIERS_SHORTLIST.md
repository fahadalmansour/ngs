# Good Suppliers Shortlist

Date: 2026-05-01

This is the practical starting list. Use these before random marketplace sellers.

## Start First

### Digital Gift Cards, Software Cards, Subscriptions, Top-Ups

1. LikeCard Business
   - Use for: KSA/MENA digital cards, gaming cards, prepaid vouchers, app-store cards, streaming cards.
   - Entity: Saudi company first.
   - Why: MENA-focused digital product platform with Riyadh presence and business/partner path.
   - First action: Apply as partner/merchant and ask for catalog export with wholesale cost, region, SKU, currency, and API access.
   - URL: https://likecard.com/business

2. Reloadly
   - Use for: global gift cards, airtime, data bundles, digital goods API.
   - Entity: Saudi or US.
   - Why: API/catalog approach is faster and cleaner than manual digital-code sourcing.
   - First action: Create account or book demo, export available Saudi/US/Global catalog, check fees and settlement currency.
   - URL: https://www.reloadly.com/reseller/

3. Infinivoucher
   - Use for: MENA game top-ups, PUBG, Free Fire, Roblox, Razer Gold, PlayStation, Google Play, Apple/iTunes, Netflix.
   - Entity: Saudi or US.
   - Why: B2B engine aimed at MENA resellers with API/catalog positioning.
   - First action: Apply for platform access and request reseller catalog export.
   - URL: https://infinivoucher.com/

4. G2Bulk
   - Use for: fast game top-up and gift-card price checks.
   - Entity: Saudi or US.
   - Why: Clear reseller/top-up positioning and API claims.
   - Caution: Crypto-only payment according to the site, so treat as backup until compliance/payment method is acceptable.
   - First action: Create test account, export product catalog if available, verify refund and region-lock terms.
   - URL: https://g2bulk.com/

5. Skarla / LikeCard ecosystem
   - Use for: prepaid gift-card and e-voucher management.
   - Entity: Saudi company.
   - Why: LikeCard ecosystem connection; useful if you later issue or distribute your own voucher products.
   - First action: Request demo and ask whether they support reseller procurement or only issuer/distribution workflows.
   - URL: https://skarla.com/

## Saudi Hardware And Electronics

1. Ingram Micro Saudi Arabia
   - Use for: IT hardware, networking, software, authorized distribution.
   - Entity: Saudi company.
   - Why: Local distributor relationship, Saudi invoice, better warranty/compliance path.
   - First action: Apply as partner and ask for price list by categories: networking, homelab/server, security, software.
   - URL: https://ingrammicrosaudi.com/become-a-partner/

2. Zeta Technologies
   - Use for: Saudi consumer electronics distribution.
   - Entity: Saudi company.
   - Why: KSA electronics distributor with major-retailer relationships shown on its site.
   - First action: Email sales requesting wholesale catalog, brands, MOQ, and reseller terms.
   - URL: https://zeta.sa/index.html

3. Ashtel KSA
   - Use for: IT, communications, electronics, multimedia, gaming/accessories, wholesale and brand distribution.
   - Entity: Saudi company.
   - Why: Saudi wholesale/distribution activity and broad electronics product fit.
   - First action: Contact corporate sales and ask for reseller onboarding plus category price list.
   - URL: https://www.ashtelksa.com/

## US Hardware And Electronics

1. Ingram Micro US
   - Use for: IT hardware, software, networking, endpoint products.
   - Entity: Wyoming company.
   - Why: Major US distributor with customer/partner onboarding.
   - First action: Apply as customer using the Wyoming company, then request category price exports.
   - URL: https://www.ingrammicro.com/en-us/sign-up/sign-up

2. TD SYNNEX US
   - Use for: IT hardware, consumer electronics, networking, security, software, infrastructure.
   - Entity: Wyoming company.
   - Why: Major US distribution channel; useful for reseller account and quote workflows.
   - First action: Apply as reseller/customer and ask for catalog/price feed access.
   - URL: https://www.tdsynnex.com/us/en/td-synnex.html

3. D&H Distributing
   - Use for: SMB IT, consumer electronics, gaming, home networking, reseller/retail channel.
   - Entity: Wyoming company.
   - Why: North American technology distributor focused on resellers and retailers.
   - First action: Apply as D&H customer and request categories matching NeoGen: gaming, networking, home automation, storage.
   - URL: https://www.dandh.com/

4. Exertis Almo
   - Use for: Pro AV, displays, projectors, creator/commercial equipment.
   - Entity: Wyoming company.
   - Why: Good specialist channel for AV and commercial equipment; not the main source for every SKU.
   - First action: Apply only if you plan to carry AV/display/projector/creator gear.
   - URL: https://www.exertisalmo.com/about

5. B&H Photo
   - Use for: creator gear, cameras, AV, specialist networking references, occasional buying.
   - Entity: Wyoming company.
   - Why: Useful reference and buying source, but not always wholesale.
   - First action: Use for benchmark and availability first; use as supplier only when margin still works after landed cost.
   - URL: https://www.bhphotovideo.com/

## Use As Market Reference, Not Main Supplier

- Amazon.sa: good local benchmark, occasional fallback, not usually a wholesale source.
- Noon: good local benchmark, occasional fallback, seller quality varies.
- AliExpress/Alibaba: useful for discovery and accessories, but every listing must be manually verified for authenticity, warranty, shipping, and region compatibility.

## First Contact Template

Subject: Reseller account and product price list request

Hello,

I operate an ecommerce business selling electronics and digital products in Saudi Arabia, with a US company for international procurement and a Saudi company for local sales/import operations.

I would like to apply for a reseller/partner account and request:

- Product catalog export
- Wholesale price list
- Stock availability feed
- MOQ and payment terms
- Shipping options to Saudi Arabia or US consolidation address
- API access if available
- Brand/region restrictions

Please let me know the documents required for onboarding.

Regards,
Fahad Almansour
